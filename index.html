Projects



Ryssa Through Time

Written and run in C++ on Unreal Engine 5 and Visual Studio

[Class Team Assignment]

In Ryssa Through Time, players become Ryssa, embarking on a quest to recover the fractured pieces of Nyxi’s Time Relic, unlocking access to the ancient dragon trials. Each trial presents a unique mix of puzzles and combats that test both mind and might. As she masters the elements of fire, ice, wind, and time, Ryssa’s power grows - but so does the threat of Vaxes, whose dark reign threatens to unravel all Elydria. The choices players make and the trials they overcome will decide whether Ryssa becomes the world’s savior or its final echo.

Some of my responsibilities included the main player characters locomotion and attributes like the attack Abilities, Animations, Mini Map, Ability Wheel, Stamina and Mana Bars, as well as debugging in other areas.

Crop Protectors

Written and run in C# on Unity

[Class Team Assignment]

Crop Protectors is a First-Person shooter where you need to defend your field of crops from hordes of hungry animals. Plant and harvest your crops to make money to upgrade your equipment, and collect power-ups to get a leg up on the critters!

In this game I worked mainly on the entire crop lifecycle system as well as the player and enemies crop interaction mechanics. This includes planting crops, being unable to harvest until fully grown, visual crop growth cycle, harvest system / player interaction harvest, damage and destruction of crops, visual indicators of crop damage, and any crop mechanic not mentioned previously. Other responsibilities include day and night cycle in endless mode, level layout, flashlight addition in endless mode, and general debugging where needed.

Behind The Lines

Written and run in C++ on Visual Studio

[Class Team Assignment]

Blocks fall from the sky as you try to clear lines, but an enemy keeps touching your pieces. The more he touches, the more he takes. It's Tetris with a saboteur messing up your perfect score. Destroy him to stop him, but keep an eye on your falling pieces.

My Contributions to this project were all 3D assets and textures, which include the player, player projectile, enemy, level layout, pumpkin button, and left and right arrow buttons made with Blender. I was also involved with the player movement mechanics, player shooting, enemy movement and splitting mechanics, block damage visual feedback, enemy and block interaction, block rotation, button interactions, etc.
